Our story

It's okay to miss home is a campaign under MUISS. We understand that homesickness is one of the problems that international students usually face, especially at Christmas.

We are a team committed to creating, forging and enhancing a better sense of belonging among international students. Therefore, our work will not be limited to one semester, but will span years to strengthen it.

About us

Mission

To connect the group value of diversity to allow the broader international student community to get involved in a mental health initiative.

Vision

Ensure that 40+ international students enjoy the gathering before the summer term ends, as well as maintaining their wellbeing consistently over the next semesters.

Value

Connectivity as the key value to ensure that we bond as one community, regardless of difference.

The Christmas Table

A free multicultural cooking workshop for students who can't get home this Christmas.

About the table

Maybe it's exams. Maybe it's work, or your own career goals. Whatever keeps you in Australia this December, there's a seat for you.

As a gathering that embraces cultural diversity, we value your unique identity, and we start with the taste of it.

  1. Bring

    One ingredient that reminds you of home. Drop it into the mystery box.

  2. Cook

    Draw three ingredients with your table, guess where they're from, and turn them into a dish nobody has cooked before.

  3. Share

    Sit down together at one long table for a Christmas meal.

  4. Keep

    Every recipe and story goes into the Recipe Zine: a keepsake to take with you and share with your family at home.

Why does it matter

At the end of the year, home means a table full of food and warm laughter. But here Christmas lands in the middle of the summer break. The campus goes quiet and family photos miss your face. If your heart feels a little heavy, please remember you are not alone. Sometimes, missing home is simply craving a familiar taste, a touch of your mum's seasoning or a childhood holiday treat. Bring that taste with you, share your story, and sit down with us.

Missing home isn't a sign of weakness, it just shows how deeply you care about a place you love. This Christmas, you don't have to carry that feeling by yourself.

How it works

Everything you need to know is inside the box. Open it, then pick a card.

Mystery box

Four cards are waiting inside

Tap the box to lift the lid. Each card explains one part of the day: how to start, what to bring, what we provide and the rules of the table.

Card 1 · How to start

One ingredient. Nobody knows what they'll get.

Bring one ingredient that tastes like home and drop it into the box. A jar of sambal might end up next to Sichuan peppercorns and a packet of dried mushrooms. Your table's job is to turn them into one dish, and learn where each ingredient comes from along the way.

Card 2 · What to bring

A small taste of home

  • One ingredient that reminds you of home: a spice, sauce, paste, tea, dried noodles or a snack.
  • Shelf-stable please, so nothing needs a fridge.
  • Sealed, with your ingredient tag attached (you'll get it when you sign up).

Card 3 · We'll provide

Everything else is on us

  • A shared pantry: rice, noodles, eggs, tofu, seasonal vegetables, oil, salt and basic sauces.
  • Cooking stations, utensils and an experienced volunteer at every table.

Card 4 · The rules

Five rules for the table

  1. Each table draws three ingredients from the box.
  2. Before cooking, guess where each one comes from. Then the owner tells you the real story.
  3. Use all three ingredients, plus anything from the shared pantry.
  4. You have 60 minutes to cook one dish.
  5. Give your dish a name that mixes its origins, and write the recipe card.

Next: FAQ

FAQ

I can't cook. Can I still come?

Yes. Cooking skill isn't required. Every table has a volunteer who'll help, so you can enjoy the meal and a warm conversation.

What ingredient should I bring?

Anything shelf-stable that reminds you of home: a spice, sauce, paste, tea or snack. Seal it and attach your ingredient tag so we can match it to everyone's dietary needs.

What if I have allergies or dietary needs?

Tell us when you sign up. Every ingredient is labelled, and tables are matched so everyone can eat what they cook.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes, if they're also spending Christmas in Australia. Ask them to sign up too, so we can save them a seat.

Will my photo or story be shared?

Only if you say yes. You choose on the sign-up form, and you'll get a "no photos" sticker on arrival if you'd rather not be photographed.

I can't make it. Can I still take part?

Yes. Send us a recipe from home and the story behind it, and we'll include it in the zine.

What if the day gets emotional?

We hear you. Missing home is what this day is about. There's a quiet space at the venue, and someone from a wellbeing service will be there to listen and talk.
